Our mission

A connected pathway into digital work

Digital Skills Platform brings practical learning, portfolio development, freelance opportunities, and mentorship together for young people in Freetown.

Built around the journey, not a single task

Young people preparing for digital work often need to move between separate learning, portfolio, marketplace, and mentoring services. This platform provides those essential steps in one clear experience.

Freelancers can learn and demonstrate their abilities, clients can discover emerging talent, and mentors can guide learners as they develop professional confidence.

Formal project title

Digital Skills and Freelancing Platform for Youth in Sierra Leone: A Case Study of Youth in Freetown.
